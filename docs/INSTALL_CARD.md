# Install card — two motions, nothing droppable

Print this and keep it with the parts. It fits on one side.

---

## Before you go: the pole gauge

Slide `pole_gauge_coupon` down the pole. It stops at the first step smaller than
the pole. **Count the ruler grooves up from the bottom** (wide groove every fifth)
to the step that stopped it — that reading picks your shim stack:

| Gauge reads | Shim stack per shell |
|---|---|
| 65.0 mm or larger | none |
| 64.0–64.5 mm | 0.5 mm |
| 63.0–63.5 mm | 1.0 mm |
| 62.0–62.5 mm | 1.5 mm |
| under 62.0 mm | outside the design range — do not fit |

Put the shims in the shell bore *behind* the TPU liner, not against the pole.

---

## Fit it — two motions

**Motion 1: wrap and throw the lever.**

1. Find the pole's weld seam with a fingernail. Rotate the clamp so the seam sits
   in the liner's centre groove — this is the one thing that protects the coating.
2. Open the shells, wrap the pole at your chosen height (speaker underside lands
   ~2100 mm above ground, so the band centre sits ~2000 mm).
3. Swing the lever closed. It goes over centre with a firm click and about 38 N —
   a comfortable one-hand pull. **It should get harder, then suddenly easier.** If
   it never gets easier you have not passed centre; if it never gets hard, add a
   shim.

**Motion 2: drop the cradle in and twist.**

4. Lower the cradle's spigot into the collar. It drops in on its own weight.
5. Rotate 15° until the detent clicks. You will feel it.

That is the whole install. Nothing was held while something else was found, and
nothing can be dropped in the grass.

**Then, before the speaker goes on:** choke the tether loop around the pole
200–300 mm above the clamp and shackle the other end to the cradle lug. Gather the
slack in the shell's keeper. See `docs/TETHER_SPEC.md`.

---

## Set the hook, once

The Move 2's handle recess dimensions are not published anywhere, so the hook is
adjustable and **you** set it:

6. Sit the speaker on the tray, pushed back against the hook post.
7. Slide the hook up or down until the TPU nose sits in the handle recess and bears
   up under the moulded lip. Snug, not tight — it is a shear stop, not a clamp.
8. Lock both M5 bolts. Mark the position with a pen; you will not have to find it
   again.

## Set the pitch

9. Pull the index pin, tilt to one of the six positions, push the pin back.

Positions are **−20, −15, −10, −5, 0, +5°**. There is no seventh hole: Sonos
permits 20° down and 5° up, and the plate has no position outside that. Inverted
use is prohibited and is not reachable.

---

## Teardown — under 30 s

Pull the index pin → twist the cradle 15° back → lift it out → unclip the tether →
throw the lever open → lift the clamp off the pole. No tools. Nothing left behind,
nothing marked.

---

## Two things that are not optional

- **The tether goes around the POLE, not the clamp.** Both ends on the clamp means
  a clamp release takes the tether with it.
- **The charging base is rated indoor only.** Module B (the USB-C retainer) is the
  weather-tolerant option and is what ships here. Do not put the factory base out
  overnight without the Module A canopy, which is not built yet.
